## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

```
Math Adventure is a calm, colourful arithmetic game for children aged 6 to 9. No
ads, no account, no internet needed — just practice and a bit of fun.

TWO GAMES

🔗 Number Chains
Solve the sum on the big number pad. Get it right and confetti bursts, you earn
a star, and the answer flies up to become the first number of the next problem.
So 3 + 4 = 7 is followed by 7 − 2 = … a chain that keeps going, with your child
building on their own last answer every time.

✂️ Splitting
Number bonds, the way they are taught at school: 10 = 4 + ? Pick 5, 10, or
"surprise" (a random number from 3 to 10). The diagram shows the number breaking
into two parts, and the side that is filled in keeps swapping, so both
directions get practised. The goal is ten correct answers in a row, with a
golden trophy at the end.

GROWS WITH YOUR CHILD

• Set the ceiling yourself, anywhere from 5 to 100. No answer ever goes above it.
• Choose which operations come up: plus, minus, times, divide — one at a time or
  mixed together.
• Settings are remembered, so you only have to get them right once.

GENTLE WITH BEGINNERS

A wrong answer costs nothing. The mascot gives a friendly little shake and your
child can try again straight away. No time pressure, no countdown clock, no
lives to lose, no "game over". In Splitting, stars already earned stay earned,
even after a miss.

FOUR LANGUAGES

Dutch, English, French and Spanish, switchable in the settings. Useful for
bilingual households and for children just starting a new language: they already
know the maths, so the words fall into place on their own.

FOR PARENTS

• No advertising, ever.
• No in-app purchases. You pay once and that is it.
• No account, no sign-in, no email address.
• No tracking and no analytics. The app collects nothing.
• No internet connection required — the app does not even ask for internet
  permission. Works in the car, on the train and in the waiting room.
• No outbound links, no social media, no chat.

Stars and settings are stored on the device and nowhere else.

FOR PHONE AND TABLET

The whole app scales to the screen and works in both portrait and landscape. On
a tablet the number pad sits beside the sum, comfortably within reach of small
hands.
```
