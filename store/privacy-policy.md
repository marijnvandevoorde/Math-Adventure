# Privacy Policy — Rekenavontuur / Math Adventure

**Last updated: 7 August 2026**

Original draft. The published, canonical policy is
https://small-victories.co/projects/math-adventure/privacy; edit that page, not this file.

---

## The short version

Rekenavontuur collects nothing, sends nothing, and needs no account. The app has
no network access at all.

## What the app stores

Two things, both kept on your device only, in the app's own private storage:

- **Settings** — the highest number to practise with, which operations are
  turned on, and the chosen language.
- **Progress** — the star count and the current run in the Splitting game.

Nothing here identifies you or your child. It never leaves the device and is not
readable by any other app. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

If you have device backup switched on (Google One / iCloud), this data may be
included in that backup, under your own account and your platform's privacy
terms. We have no access to those backups.

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts, sign-in, or email addresses.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage measurement.
- No third-party SDKs of any kind.
- No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or file access.
- No links out of the app, no social features, no chat, no user-generated
  content.
- No in-app purchases.

## Network access

The Android build declares **no `INTERNET` permission**, so the operating system
prevents it from making network connections at all. The iOS build makes no
network requests. The entire game is bundled inside the app and served locally.

## Children

The app is aimed at children aged 6 to 9 and is designed so there is nothing to
collect. It complies with COPPA and the GDPR by not gathering personal data in
the first place — there is no data to request, correct, export, or delete beyond
uninstalling the app.
