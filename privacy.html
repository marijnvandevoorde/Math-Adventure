Privacy Policy

Math Adventure / Rekenavontuur

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Math Adventure is made by Small Victories BV (Belgium) and published on Google Play under the package name co.smallvictories.mathadventure.

This policy is short because there is very little to say: Math Adventure collects nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.

What leaves the device

Nothing. Math Adventure has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no accounts. The Android app declares no permissions whatsoever — including no INTERNET permission, which means the operating system itself prevents it from making any network connection. The entire game ships inside the download and is read from local storage while you play.

A practical consequence: Math Adventure works exactly the same in the car, on a train, on a plane, or on a tablet that has never been online.

What is stored on your device

Math Adventure remembers a few things locally so it behaves sensibly between sessions:

  • The number of stars earned.
  • The highest number setting and which operations are enabled.
  • The chosen language.

These live in the app's private storage on your own device. They are not an account, they are not tied to you or your child, and they are never transmitted. Uninstalling Math Adventure deletes all of them.

What Math Adventure does not do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking of any kind.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No location, microphone, camera or contacts access.
  • No network access of any kind.
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment data — Math Adventure is a one-off purchase handled entirely by Google Play, which never shares your payment details with us.
  • No links out of the app, no social media, no chat, no user-generated content.

Children

Math Adventure is designed for children aged roughly 6 to 9. It does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all and cannot transmit data off the device.

Your rights

Because Math Adventure holds no personal data and transmits nothing, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete — we have never received anything to begin with. To remove the app's local data (stars and settings), uninstalling Math Adventure wipes it from the device.
