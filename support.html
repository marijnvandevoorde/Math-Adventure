Support

Math Adventure / Rekenavontuur

Something not working, or a question about the app? Email hello@small-victories.co and we will get back to you. There is no account to recover and no subscription to cancel — Math Adventure is a one-time purchase that runs entirely on your device.

Changing what your child practises

Tap the ⚙️ button on the home screen. There you can set how high the numbers go (anywhere from 5 to 100 — no answer ever goes above the ceiling you pick), and choose which operations come up: plus, minus, times and divide, one at a time or mixed together. Settings are remembered, so you only have to set them once.

Changing the language

Also under ⚙️. Math Adventure speaks Dutch, English, French and Spanish, and switches instantly. The maths is identical in every language, which makes it a gentle way into a new one.

Stars and progress

Stars are stored on the device and nowhere else. They do not sync between devices, because the app has no network access at all. Deleting the app removes them, and reinstalling starts from zero.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The whole game ships inside the app, so it works in the car, on the train and in the waiting room. The app has no network access whatsoever — see the privacy policy.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Neither, and there never will be. You pay once. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics, no chat and no outbound links.

A wrong answer

Costs nothing. The mascot gives a friendly shake and your child can try again straight away — no time pressure, no countdown, no lives, no “game over”. In Splitting, stars already earned stay earned.

Reporting a problem

Email hello@small-victories.co. It helps to mention the device (for example “iPad, iPadOS 18”), the app version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot is worth a lot.
